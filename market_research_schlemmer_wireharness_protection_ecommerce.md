## 1. Market Research Findings

### 1.1 Company Overview: Schlemmer Group

**History and Background**

Schlemmer Group is a German industrial manufacturer specializing in cable protection systems and wire harness components, with roots dating back to 1954. The company was founded in Munich, Germany, and has grown over decades into a recognized global supplier in the automotive and industrial cable protection space.

**Headquarters:** Poing, near Munich, Germany (historically also associated with Hauzenberg, Bavaria for manufacturing operations).

**Global Presence:**
- Operations spanning Europe, Asia, and the Americas
- Manufacturing facilities in Germany, Czech Republic, China, India, Brazil, Mexico, and the United States
- Approximately 20+ locations worldwide across production, sales, and logistics
- Estimated workforce of 2,000–3,000 employees globally

**Ownership History:**
- Originally a family-owned German Mittelstand company
- Acquired by 3i Group (British private equity) in the mid-2010s
- Filed for insolvency in 2020 amid financial difficulties exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic and existing debt burdens
- Subsequently restructured and acquired by MUTARES SE & Co. KGaA, a Munich-based private equity firm focused on turnaround situations (2020–2021)
- Under Mutares, the company has undergone restructuring aimed at stabilizing operations and returning to profitability

**Key Facts:**
- Annual revenue estimated in the range of EUR 200–350 million (post-restructuring)
- Primarily serves the automotive sector (~70–80% of revenue), with growing industrial and infrastructure segments
- Holds numerous patents in corrugated tubing and cable protection technology
- Certified to IATF 16949 (automotive quality), ISO 9001, and ISO 14001 standards

### 1.2 Product Portfolio

Schlemmer offers one of the broadest product ranges in the wire harness protection industry:

#### A. Corrugated Tubing (Core Product Line — Flagship)
- **Standard Corrugated Tubing (PA/PP/PE):** Available in polyamide (PA6, PA12), polypropylene (PP), and polyethylene (PE). Diameters range from NW 4.5 mm to NW 70 mm+
- **SCS (Schlemmer Corrugated System):** Integrated corrugated tubing system with matching connectors
- **High-temperature corrugated tubing:** Rated up to 150°C+, designed for engine bay applications
- **Split corrugated tubing:** Pre-slit tubing for retrofit/aftermarket installation over existing cables
- **Flame-retardant variants:** Meeting UL94 V0/V2 standards
- **Thin-wall corrugated tubing:** Lightweight versions for weight-sensitive EV platforms

#### B. Connectors and Fittings
- Straight connectors, angled connectors (45°, 90°), Y-connectors, T-connectors
- Reducer connectors for diameter transitions
- Sealing connectors (IP54, IP67, IP68 rated)
- Quick-connect/snap-in fittings

#### C. Clips and Fastening Systems
- Corrugated tubing clips, adhesive-backed clips, push-in clips, cable tie mounts

#### D. Grommets and Sealing Systems
- Bulkhead grommets, single and multi-entry grommets, rubber/TPE sealing elements

#### E. Braided Sleeving and Textile Protection
- PET expandable braided sleeving, woven textile wraps, heat-shrink tubing

#### F. Specialty and System Solutions
- Complete cable protection routing systems (end-to-end)
- EV-specific solutions for high-voltage battery connections and charging systems
- Underbody protection systems for harsh under-vehicle environments

#### G. Key Product Series
- **SCG Series:** Standard corrugated conduit range
- **SCS System:** Schlemmer Connection System (integrated tubing + connector platform)
- **HTS (High Temperature Series):** Elevated-temperature applications
- **EVO Series:** Lighter-weight, next-generation designs targeting EV applications

### 1.8 Emerging Trends

#### Electric Vehicle Impact (Transformative)
- EVs contain ~3–5 km of wiring vs. 1–3 km in ICE vehicles
- High-voltage cable protection (400V–800V) requires enhanced insulation, EMI shielding, thermal management
- EV transition expected to increase addressable market by **30–50% per vehicle by 2030**

#### Lightweighting
- Thin-wall corrugated tubing, optimized materials, topology-optimized connectors
- Weight reduction targets of 15–30% vs. conventional tubing

#### Sustainability & Circular Economy
- Growing demand for recycled-content materials (PCR polyamide/polypropylene)
- Bio-based polymers (PA11 from castor oil)
- Carbon footprint declarations increasingly required by OEMs
- Delfingen has been particularly aggressive in sustainability marketing

#### Industry 4.0 & Digitalization
- Digital twins for cable harness routing simulation
- AI-driven demand forecasting and automatic replenishment
- QR codes and RFID tags for supply chain traceability
- Smart conduits with embedded sensors (early-stage)

#### Disruptive Forces
- Chinese competition rapidly improving quality
- Vertical integration by harness makers (Yazaki, Sumitomo, Leoni producing more in-house)
- Zonal architecture shift may reduce total wiring in long term
- Wireless connectivity gradually replacing some low-voltage wiring

### 2.7 Key Analytical Insights

1. **The E-Commerce Paradox:** Schlemmer's strongest advantages (OEM approvals, automotive-grade quality) are least valued in the segments where e-commerce grows fastest. Bridge through content marketing, application-specific kitting, and selective sub-branding.

2. **Kitting Is the E-Commerce Strategy:** Individual tubing sold by the meter is a commodity on Amazon. Application-specific kits are defensible, higher-margin, and harder for Chinese competitors to replicate.

3. **Sustainability Is the Pricing Moat:** In a commoditizing market, sustainability credentials are one of the few defensible sources of price premium. European manufacturing = lower logistics carbon vs. China.

4. **The EV Tailwind Is Real but Requires Investment:** 30–50% increase in wire harness content per BEV is structural. Capturing the EV aftermarket requires new product development and marketing investment.
